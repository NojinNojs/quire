# Quire design brief

The current direction is [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md): a task-first reading workspace with a single light palette, cover-led collections and clear reading controls.

The dark glass concept from 28 October 2026 was cancelled. It is not a source for current colors, typography, motion or components. The [design decision](../adr/0001-paper-editorial-not-glass.md) records that change; subsequent work refined the palette and shell.
