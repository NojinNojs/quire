# Cover sources

These are downloaded images of published books, not generated artwork. They are bundled
locally for the Quire demo. Cover artwork remains attributed to its original publishers,
designers, and rights holders. The edition metadata in the demo is illustrative.

Images were visually checked against their book titles, resized without enlargement, and
encoded as WebP. `object-fit: contain` preserves the entire cover. The Histories uses a
publisher cover rather than the title-page scan returned by the catalogue's default result.

`placeholder.svg` is one shared neutral missing-cover asset, drawn as a simple book outline
with the label “No cover available.” It contains no invented book art or per-title rendering.
